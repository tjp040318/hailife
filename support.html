Common questions

How do I cancel or manage my HaiLife Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → HaiLife, or in the App Store app tap your profile photo → Subscriptions. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: You tab → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This wipes the data on your phone right away and removes your account, cloud-stored data and sign-in identity from our servers within 30 days (see the Privacy Policy for the one code we keep to limit free weeks). Uninstalling the app also removes all local data from your device.

Why isn't my ride syncing to Strava?

Open the You tab and check that Strava shows as connected. If it disconnected, reconnect and the app will sync new activities automatically. Make sure you granted upload permission when authorizing Strava.

The app can't read my heart rate / health data

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → HaiLife and enable the data types you want to share. HaiLife only reads what you allow.

Is my data private?

Yes — see the Privacy Policy. Highlights: while you're signed in, your account is backed up to our cloud service so a new phone doesn't lose it; AI coaching only sends data after you explicitly consent in-app; we never sell your data.

The AI coach isn't responding

Check your internet connection first. AI features also need Pro (or your free week, or your own AI key) and the one-time AI consent (Coach tab). If it still fails, force-quit the app, reopen, and try again — then email us if the problem persists.
